# BOQ, QTO, Cutting List, Cost, VE, Procurement

## QTO method
SOURCE → ITEM → DIMENSION → FORMULA → REPETITION → NET QTY → UNIT → WASTE → PROCUREMENT QTY. No unexplained quantity. Keep net, waste, allowance and contingency in separate columns; never hide waste inside net.

Break down: main materials, substrates, profiles, hardware, fixings, accessories, consumables, finishes, supports, electrical components. Steel: lengths, sections, unit weights, counts, total weights separately. Sheets: stock size, grain direction, kerf, edge allowance, nesting, offcuts, purchase quantity.

Table: | Item | Description | Material | Dimensions | Qty | Unit | Calculation Basis | Waste | Total | Source |

Cutting list: | Item | Material | Thickness | W | L | Qty | Board size | Waste | Remarks |. Prefer standard commercial sizes; respect minimum practical piece size and joint locations.

## Cost
Separate: material, fabrication, labor, hardware, transport, equipment, installation, waste, subcontractor, overhead, contingency. Label each price class (verified / quotation / market / budgetary / assumption). Do not fabricate supplier prices. Search current Saudi market data when pricing matters and cite it.

## Value engineering
Goal: same design intent + acceptable performance + lower TOTAL cost (system, not just material). Levers: material substitution, thickness optimization, simplified fabrication, standardization, modularity, fewer layers, simpler fixing, less hidden structure, alternative finish/graphics, local fabrication, standard hardware. Never cut safety, fire, structural adequacy, durability, accessibility, required appearance, maintainability.

Comparison: | Criteria | Existing | Alt A | Alt B | Recommended | with rows: material cost, fabrication, installation, durability, appearance, availability, maintenance, risk, overall cost.

## Procurement
Standard sizes, local availability, lead time, MOQ, special-order items, replacement availability, fabrication capability. Prefer commercially standard products unless project requires otherwise. Flag long-lead items.
