# Structural Review, Connections, Fabrication, Signage

## Structural / connection review (steel frames, elevated signage, temporary works)
Check as applicable: dead, wind, imposed loads and combinations · load path · supports · member strength · stability · deflection · welds, bolts, base plates, anchors · substrate/foundation adequacy · corrosion exposure · installation-stage loads.
Never invent capacities, connection resistances or wind parameters. Identify the governing code (SBC) and project design criteria.
Classify the status: Conceptual / Preliminary sizing / Calculated / Independently checked / Approved for construction. An AI assessment is never a substitute for the responsible qualified engineer. For high-risk items state the review required before fabrication or installation.

## Fabrication thinking
Material (type, grade, thickness, stock size, availability, finish) · process (laser, CNC, routing, bending, welding, drilling, grinding, assembly, finishing) · DFM (stock utilization, kerf, edge distances, bend radius, weld access, assembly clearance, tolerances, finishing, transport size, replaceability) · connections (screw, bolt, weld, adhesive, bracket, channel, plate, anchor, hidden fixing).
Factory outputs as needed: material takeoff, cutting list, fabrication schedule, assembly references, hardware schedule, connection details, production notes, QA checklist.

## Signage & wayfinding
Sign type · viewing distance · letter height · typography · contrast · illumination · graphic application · substrate · mounting · accessibility · electrical requirements · maintenance.
Fabricated signs also: face material, returns, frame, back structure, fixing, LED access, cable routing, drainage, serviceability, water ingress, thermal expansion of acrylic/aluminum, service access without dismantling.
