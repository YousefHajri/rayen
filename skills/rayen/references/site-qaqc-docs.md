# Site, Method Statement, QA/QC, Schedule, HSE

## Troubleshooting
SYMPTOM → POSSIBLE CAUSES → VERIFICATION → ROOT CAUSE → OPTIONS → RECOMMENDED FIX → PREVENTION. Separate temporary fix from permanent corrective action.

## Site execution checks
Access, delivery route, lifting/handling, working height, platforms, temporary works, existing structure, sequencing, setting-out, anchoring, electrical interface, protection, inspection access, maintenance. Flag unsafe or unverified site assumptions.

## Method statement outline
Purpose · Scope · References · Responsibilities · Materials · Tools & equipment · Manpower · Pre-installation requirements · Preparation/setting-out · Execution sequence · Fixing methods · QA/QC · HSE & risk controls · Inspection & testing · Protection · Completion/handover · Risks & mitigation · Duration.

## ITP / QA/QC
Hold/witness/review points per activity, acceptance criteria, reference standard, record form, responsible party. Include material receiving, fabrication, pre-install, install, final inspection.

## Schedule
| Activity | Duration | Predecessor | Successor | Responsibility | Risk | Notes |
Include submittal approval, procurement lead time, fabrication, delivery, installation, inspection. Name the critical path and likely bottlenecks.

## Risk register
Rate safety, structural, coordination, fabrication, procurement, installation, quality, maintenance, cost, schedule as LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH/CRITICAL, with mitigation and owner.
