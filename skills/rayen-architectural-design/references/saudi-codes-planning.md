# Saudi codes, authorities, climate, identity  (verify editions per project)

## Saudi Building Code (SBC) map — identify the part, then the edition adopted for the permit
| Part | Scope | Typical use for RAYEN |
|---|---|---|
| SBC 201 | General building code (IBC-based): occupancy, construction type, heights/areas, egress (ch.10), accessibility (ch.11), interior finishes (ch.8) | Space planning, exits, accessible design |
| SBC 301–306 | Structural: loads (301 — wind, seismic, live), concrete, masonry, steel | Signage/pylon wind loads (via structural engineer) |
| SBC 401 | Electrical | Sign lighting, LED screens, earthing |
| SBC 501 | Mechanical | HVAC interface |
| SBC 601 / 602 | Energy conservation: non-residential / residential | Envelope U-values, glazing SHGC, LPD |
| SBC 701 / 702 | Plumbing / private sewage | Fixture counts, wet cores |
| SBC 801 | Fire protection (IFC-based) | Sprinklers, alarms, finishes class, signs |
| SBC 1101 / 1102 | Residential building codes (verify which part covers the project) | Villas, housing |
Plus: Civil Defense requirements, Balady (MOMRAH) building-permit and signage (لوحات) regulations, municipality advertising rules, Saudi Universal Accessibility guidelines, special-zone design codes (Diriyah, AlUla/RCU, NEOM, RCRC/King Salman Park, Red Sea…), project spec, and Mostadam / LEED if targeted.
Never state "compliant" — state "requires verification against SBC <part> (<edition>) and authority".

## Riyadh climate design (hot-arid)
Summer > 45 °C, large diurnal range, low humidity, dust storms, high UV, rare but intense rain.
- Orientation: long façades N/S; minimise E/W glazing; deep reveals, fins on E/W, horizontal shading on S.
- Thermal mass + insulation; light, high-reflectance roofs; shaded courtyards and mashrabiya-like screens (with daylight & view balance).
- Glazing: low-e, low SHGC, keep WWR moderate; avoid unshaded skylights.
- Dust: sealed joints, maintainable finishes, avoid dust-trap horizontal ledges at signage; access for cleaning.
- Materials: UV-stable (PVDF coatings, UV-stabilised acrylic), allow thermal expansion (acrylic ≈ 7×10⁻⁵ /K, aluminium ≈ 2.3×10⁻⁵ /K — design joints accordingly).

## Saudi architectural identity
- **Saudi Architecture Map / King Salman Charter for Architecture & Urbanism**: national reference for regional characters (Najdi, Hejazi, Asiri, Eastern/Al-Ahsa, Northern, Madinah, etc.) — TO BE CONFIRMED: which character applies to the project's city and whether compliance is mandatory for the facade (some municipalities enforce it for commercial streets).
- **Najdi (Riyadh) language**: solid mass, small deep openings, triangular (zigzag) parapet crenellations, courtyards, earth tones, mud-plaster texture, palm-trunk/tamarisk ceilings, light slots.
- **Contemporary Salmani reading**: abstract the Najdi rules — proportion, solidity, shade lines, rhythm of openings, material honesty, modern materials (GRC, stone, metal screens) — no kitsch.
- Signage on heritage-character façades: integrate in a defined sign band, restrained palette, back-lit or halo-lit letters, no over-sized boxes.

## Permits & authority workflow (typical)
Concept → municipality / Balady pre-check (zoning, setbacks, use) → architectural + structural + MEP drawings by a licensed office → Civil Defense → permit → construction supervision → occupancy. Advertising signs require separate Balady sign licence (size, height, illumination, location). Confirm current steps on Balady portal — procedures change.
