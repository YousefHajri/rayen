## Typical materials & where they fail
| Material | Good for | Watch |
|---|---|---|
| Porcelain tiles (R10–R11 wet) | High traffic | Lippage, movement joints every ~6–8 m and at perimeters |
| Marble / granite | Lobbies, counters | Staining (seal), slip when polished in wet areas, weight on joinery |
| Terrazzo / quartz | Counters, floors | Seams, heat, adhesive compatibility |
| Vinyl / LVT | Offices, retail | Substrate flatness, heat under glazing |
| Carpet tiles | Offices | Acoustic benefit; fire class & smoke |
| Engineered wood / veneer | Feature walls, joinery | Humidity, UV fading, fire class (fire-retardant boards where required) |
| HPL / compact laminate | Joinery, WC cubicles | Edge detail, exposed core colour |
| Lacquered MDF (HMR) | Joinery | Chipping at edges; use 2–3 mm radius; avoid in wet zones unless HMR + sealed |
| Gypsum board (std / MR / FR) | Ceilings, partitions | Joints, access, MR in wet areas, FR for rated walls |
| Acoustic panels / baffles | Meeting, F&B | NRC target, fire class, cleaning |
| Fabric | Seating, panels | Rub count (≥ 30–50k Martindale contract), fire treatment |
| Glass (tempered/laminated) | Partitions, balustrades | Safety glazing, manifestation strips, deflection |
| Acrylic | Signage, light walls | Expansion, scratching, heat from LEDs |
| Stainless / brass PVD | Trims, letters | Fingerprints (anti-fingerprint), galvanic contact |

## Fire (interior finishes)
Finish classes (A/B/C by flame spread / smoke) depend on occupancy and location (exits, corridors, rooms) per SBC 201 ch.8 / SBC 801 — tag every finish with required class and "TO BE CONFIRMED by fire consultant". Use fire-retardant MDF/plywood, FR gypsum and tested systems for rated walls/doors; seal penetrations with listed firestop.

## Acoustics (targets, typical)
Office meeting rooms partitions STC/Rw 45–50 (confidential 50+), full-height to slab or with ceiling barrier · Open office NRC ceiling ≥ 0.7 · F&B reverberation control (NRC ≥ 0.7 on ≥ 25 % surfaces) · Door seals & undercut affect STC.

## Durability & maintenance
Riyadh dust: low-texture or washable finishes in public areas; specify cleaning method. Corner guards and kick plates in BOH. Spare stock (attic stock) 2–5 % for tiles/carpet.
