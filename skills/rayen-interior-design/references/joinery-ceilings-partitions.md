# Joinery, ceilings, partitions — details that build

## Joinery (MDF / plywood / veneer / HPL / solid surface)
- Boards 1220×2440 (also 1830×3660 for some veneers/HPL — confirm). Carcass 18 mm (HMR MDF or plywood), backs 6–9 mm, shelves ≤ 900 span at 18 mm (or add stiffener), 25 mm for heavy loads.
- Edges: ABS/PVC 1–2 mm or solid lipping 6–10 mm; lacquer edges radius 2 mm.
- Hardware: soft-close hinges (35 mm cup), full-extension runners (load rating), push-to-open where handleless, lock & cable grommets Ø60/80.
- Scribes & shadow gaps: 10–20 mm scribe to walls; 10 mm shadow gaps at floor/ceiling; plinth 100 mm recessed 50–75.
- Tolerances: site walls ±10 mm out of plumb → design adjustable fixings (French cleats, Z-clips, slotted holes). Factory tolerance ±1 mm on panels.
- Panelling: Z-clip / French cleat on battens (MDF/aluminium), expansion gap at joints 3–5 mm or designed reveal; fire-retardant board where required.
- Counters: stone top support every ≤ 600 mm, overhang ≤ 300 mm unsupported (stone thickness dependent), steel sub-frame for long spans; access door for cables/PSU.
- Integrated lighting: aluminium profile with diffuser, ventilation slots for drivers, removable panel for driver access.

## Gypsum ceilings & bulkheads
- Suspended system (primary/secondary channels or proprietary system) with hangers typically ≤ 1200 c/c (follow manufacturer), board 12.5 mm (MR in wet, FR where rated).
- Access panels at every valve, damper, FCU, junction box — min 600×600 (or as MEP requires).
- Shadow gap perimeter trims, expansion/control joints ≈ every 12–15 m and at building joints.
- Cove lighting: cove depth & opening per profile; paint white inside; LED aimed to ceiling, not visible from eye level.
- Coordinate: sprinklers, detectors, diffusers, speakers, CCTV — keep a reflected-ceiling coordination sheet.

## Partitions
- Metal stud 50/70/75/92/100 mm at 600 c/c (400 for tiling/heavy fixtures), double board for acoustic/fire, insulation inside, full height to slab for STC/fire.
- Blocking/backing plates for TVs, wall-hung WCs, signage and heavy joinery — show on drawings.
- Glass partitions: frameless 10–12 mm tempered (laminated where required), floor/ceiling channels, doors with patch fittings, manifestation strips at 850–1000 and 1400–1600 AFF.

## Flooring interfaces
Transitions at doors under the leaf centreline, level difference ≤ 6 mm without bevel (accessible), movement joints at substrate joints, raised floor height for floor boxes.
